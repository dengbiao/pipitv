# Series games: first prototype

`Listen & Find` is opened with the quiet `Play Games` button beside each homepage series' topic line. The `进入` action on the right of its title opens the series list without autoplay; its cover continues to start scheduled playback. Series-list headers have no game button. Back from a video reveals its series list; Back from a list or game restores the corresponding homepage trigger. Unsupported series have no game entrance; locked games show a quiet disabled `Game locked` button. During the current testing phase, the requirement defaults to 0 (immediate access) and can be changed per series to 0–5 in the PIN-protected parent space under 系列游戏. Parents can preview games without editing the child's viewing history.

A complete round means every **currently present** video/audio file in the entry (including subfolders) has completed once. Repeating just one file cannot unlock the whole series. Images/PDFs and incomplete downloads are excluded. For positive viewing requirements, missing/unreadable storage fails closed. A requirement of 0 permits the bundled game even without playable media. Adding a new episode requires that episode to reach the same threshold. Entry roots isolate progress; statistics resets preserve game counts and review/resume history.

Historical ended-play counts are retained when migrating. New game completions require an ended event plus actual playback of at least max(95% of duration, duration minus 1.5 seconds); pause/buffering/background/seek time is excluded using the existing playback clocks. Partial watch time survives a restart. A completed exposure resets its pending watch time. These are exposure counts, not evidence of mastery.

Guli and WOW each shuffle 20 source-linked questions. Correct positions are randomized in blocks containing left, middle and right once each: over 20 questions each position is correct 6–7 times, with no three consecutive answers in the same position. Distractors are shuffled separately. Retries retain the question's original positions. The initial D-pad focus is simply the first available picture and does not indicate correctness. The small built-in demonstration decks keep their introductory lengths. It speaks the question on entry and offers Listen again. Correct answers speak “Well done!” plus the word/phrase, then automatically advance after the recording actually ends; there is no Next button. Wrong answers speak the user-specified “You are wrong. This is …”, naming the selected picture, then replay the same question. The selected picture label is revealed during feedback. Rapid OK presses cannot skip feedback. Interrupted recordings never auto-advance; Replay feedback recovers an interruption. Back stops sound and restores the game entry. The completion page offers Play again and Back to series. Child games count toward the existing daily/session allowance; parent previews do not consume child time.

## Video-grounded content

Both series were rebuilt from the actual 38 USB videos: 12 Guli episodes and 26 WOW clips, totaling about 115 minutes of original audio. Audio tracks were extracted without altering source videos, then transcribed locally. Curated question metadata includes the source video ID/name, timestamped target-word excerpt, timestamped phrase excerpt, and whether the question is an original question or an adaptation. Do not treat repeated ASR text as a precise word-frequency measurement.

- Guli: 20 questions cover greetings, sleeping frogs, ears/nose/feet, hide-and-seek/playground, filling a bucket, riding/driving, four balloons, baking a cake, picking apples/carrots/nuts, stars/owls, following a bunny, umbrellas and being a cook.
- WOW: 20 questions cover greetings/run, red/green/yellow, house/door/tree/sun, car/bike/train, arms/legs, apple/banana/orange, butterfly/bee and happy faces. They reuse or adapt actual forms such as “What is it?”, “What colour is it?”, “Where are my arms?”, “Can I have the banana, please?”, and “Are you happy?”.
- The parent games panel lists the series vocabulary. Full word lists, prompts, source clips and timestamps are in [series-vocabulary.md](series-vocabulary.md).
- Unknown new series remain disabled until an appropriate source-grounded question pack is authored. No unrelated vocabulary is silently invented.

Content lives in `public/game-content.json`. Both real-series packs now use **48 reviewed video screenshots** (22 Guli and 26 WOW), including every correct answer and distractor. Pictures stay in their own series even when words such as apple or car overlap. Body parts, characters, props and actions are cropped from original scenes; balloon quantities use real scene crops rather than repeated illustrations. Rendering preserves each crop's aspect ratio. Screenshots are bundled offline in `public/game-stills/` and need no TV connection during a game.

`scripts/game-picture-selections.json` records the reviewed source video ID, requested timestamp, normalized crop and subject description. `public/game-pictures.json` also records the source filename/byte size and output dimensions; the content builder includes this provenance as `seriesPictures`. The screenshot time is independent of the spoken-word excerpt. No source USB files are changed. The ADB-only `scripts/tools/ExtractGameFrames.java` can read candidate frames into app-independent temporary authoring storage; failed decodes were replaced by local FFmpeg extraction from read-only cached originals. Raw candidate frames and review contact sheets remain in `outputs/qa/game-video-pictures/`. Rebuild reviewed crops using Pillow with `scripts/build_game_pictures.py`, then rebuild question content.

Only the small introductory demonstration packs retain unmodified OpenMoji 15.0.0 SVGs, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0; attribution and full license are bundled under `public/game-art/ATTRIBUTION.txt` and `public/licenses/openmoji-LICENSE.txt`. Source: https://github.com/hfg-gmuend/openmoji/tree/15.0.0/color/svg .

The bundled English game clips use macOS Samantha with a **0.85× tempo**, one step (about 15%) slower than before, preserving pitch. Nearby `say` synthesis-rate settings produced identical recordings on the current Mac, so the generator keeps its original 145 wpm synthesis and applies FFmpeg's pitch-preserving tempo filter to the offline recording. Wrong feedback adds about 350 ms between “You are wrong.” and “This is …” so the selected object's name is distinct. The caption remains exactly the spoken English, with no synthesis commands in the UI. `public/game-audio/generation.json` records the voice/rate/pause profile and participates in incremental regeneration, preventing unchanged captions from retaining the old speech rate. Captions are in `public/game-audio/captions.json`. Regenerate with `python3 scripts/generate_game_audio.py`; FFmpeg can be supplied with `PIPITV_FFMPEG` or found in the existing local tools cache. Android uses native MediaPlayer and audio focus, independent of system TTS/WebView autoplay. Sound stops on exit, background, or lost audio focus. Separate rest-reminder recordings are unchanged.

Wrong picture choices use a prominent red ring and a white × badge at the top-right corner, including while that picture owns remote focus. The red state overrides the shared green focus ring. After wrong feedback finishes, the incorrect picture and its enlarged red feedback remain marked until this question is answered correctly; only its photo dims during retry. Other choices remain selectable, and no question/position changes occur on a wrong answer. Correct feedback still waits for the recording to finish before advancing.

## Preview and verification

`/game-feedback-preview.html` is a development-only, in-memory preview of the same production game component and real offline clips. It can review both packs outside child learning hours, without changing the normal preview's PIN, time settings or viewing progress. Its narrow native-bridge mock handles only speech/stop; no media catalog is replaced. The page and fixture script are excluded from the TV APK.

Run Vite on **4191**, then open `/game-preview.html`. The preview uses the actual TV USB catalog snapshot in `outputs/qa/game-resource-repair/catalog.json`: Guli has 12 videos; WOW has 26 videos and 5 PDFs. It no longer substitutes two sample episodes per series or invents completed viewing rounds. The first migration changes the old preview entries to the real paths and sets both game thresholds to 0; later parent choices persist. Browser progress/settings remain separate from the TV. Its browser-only test PIN is **2468**. The development-only `scripts/tv-preview.mjs` middleware serves only catalog-indexed originals on localhost, lazily caching a selected file via read-only ADB pull. Directory browsing does not download the whole library. Production/APK builds contain no ADB/HTTP playback dependency and keep reading the USB directly.

Native QA uses a separate package, `com.pipi.kids.gamesqa`, with the same built assets/source and disposable media/history. Fixture verification uses separate storage. The resource repair and 0-view testing default have subsequently been installed in the normal `com.pipi.kids` app, preserving its real entries, parent settings and existing viewing history. Screenshots, fixture code and results live in `outputs/qa/series-games/`.

Rebuild the curated source-linked content with `python3 scripts/build_series_game_content.py`, then regenerate changed prompts with `python3 scripts/generate_game_audio.py`. Audio extraction/transcription evidence is in `outputs/qa/series-vocabulary/`. The extraction helper is ADB-only and is never bundled in the TV application.
